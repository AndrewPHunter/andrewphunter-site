## Design Context

Aligned to the user-selected Plainsight Systems, Charlotte, Cradle, and Serenity
sites on 2026-10-12. The existing site describes this as Andrew Hunter's personal
archive of writing on architecture, delivery systems, and engineering leadership.
Serenity's saved design context documents the approved family direction.

### Users

Engineers, systems architects, and technical leaders reading Andrew's essays,
following a series, or finding the practical reasoning behind his project work.
Reading and discovering related writing are the primary tasks.

### Brand Personality

Curious, precise, personal. Preserve Andrew's existing prose and the distinction
between this personal writing site and the Plainsight research hub. As of
2026-10-15, Andrew has paused promotion of consulting work: keep consulting and
advisory service references out of public copy, navigation, and metadata until
he asks to restore them.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the established family palette: navy #0e1524, ivory #fafaf5, light ink
#f1ebdc, brass #d4ad5a. Navy full-width header and footer frame ivory reading
surfaces. Self-hosted IBM Plex Serif for display and prose, Plex Mono for small
navigation and metadata. These user-selected references take precedence over
any generic skill font exclusions. Illustration uses the family's textured
night-workshop style. Thin rules, square edges, restrained accents, no card grid.

About and Contact share the site's full outer width and responsive gutters;
keep their content aligned with the masthead and footer. The favicon distills the
writing desk into a brass lamp and open notebook. The default social card uses
the central desk scene with Andrew's name and site address.

### Design Principles

1. Make the family resemblance immediate through framing, palette, and typography.
2. Keep reading central: comfortable line lengths, useful headings, clear links.
3. Preserve the archive, series order, article URLs, and existing prose.
4. Let the personal illustration and writing give this site its own identity.
5. Support small screens, visible keyboard focus, and reduced motion.
