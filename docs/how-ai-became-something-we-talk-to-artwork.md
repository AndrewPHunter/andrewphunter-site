### Generation prompt

+Use case: editorial illustration / article cover.
Create a finished landscape sharing cover for an essay titled "How AI Became Something We Talk To". Desired canvas 1200 by 630 pixels, wide 1.905:1 landscape composition.
Art direction: a thoughtful illustrated magazine cover in a textured night-workshop aesthetic, deep ink navy #0e1524, warm ivory #fafaf5, muted brass #d4ad5a, subtle paper and print texture, beautifully drawn rather than photorealistic or slick 3D. Quietly intriguing, not alarmist. The essay is about the history of AI culminating in a useful natural language interface and how that interface imports science-fiction expectations.
Composition: large readable warm ivory editorial serif title on the left, exact text on three lines "How AI Became" / "Something" / "We Talk To". Smaller restrained byline at lower left: "Andrew Hunter". On the right a beautifully observed beige 1980s desktop CRT computer with physical keyboard on a desk, amber or pale green glowing screen. On its screen just the short conversational exchange "> Can you explain this?" then "Let's work through it." Behind the small real computer, on the navy wall, its large shadow suggests the head and shoulders of an imagined science-fiction thinking machine, a simple elegant silhouette. The shadow is clearly an imagined projection, not another physical robot. No threatening red eyes, no brand logos, no circuit-brain cliches, no floating holograms. Restrained warmth from the CRT illuminates the desk. The visual distinction is modest actual terminal versus the larger intelligence we imagine behind it. All text exact and legible. Keep all essential content within a generous 7% inset safe area so the cover remains usable in social previews. Make the title dominate and the computer/shadow read even at small size. Only the specified title, byline, and screen text. Opaque background.

## Infographic

- Method: hand-authored native SVG for precise labels and editable geometry; no image-generation model used.

- Brief: show the three meanings of AI. A broad field contains selected research approaches, with machine learning containing deep learning and large language models. A solid arrow connects the model to its natural language interface. A fictional thinking machine sits outside the map, connected to the interface by a dotted line representing a shared name and borrowed expectations. The map is illustrative, not an exhaustive taxonomy or a forecast of progress toward general intelligence.
- Palette follows the site's ivory, navy, and brass; muted green distinguishes the nested learning regions.
