I'm an Enterprise Architect and engineering leader focused on building systems that remain understandable, governable, and safe to evolve as they scale.

My work sits at the intersection of architecture and execution: clarifying boundaries, preserving constraints, and establishing governance that improves delivery without sacrificing correctness.

I write about the structural problems that appear as systems grow — distributed architectures, AI-driven inference loops, and the governance challenges of operating complex software safely.
